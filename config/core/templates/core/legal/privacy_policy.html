Privacy Policy - ZionCare Development Loan

LAST UPDATED: {% now "F j, Y" %} • EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY

At ZionCare Development Loan, your privacy and financial security are our highest priority. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and manage your information when you use our loan management system.

1. Information We Collect

  • Client Information: Full name, phone number, address, branch, loan amount, repayment history.
  • Operational Data: Daily collections, expected vs collected amounts, staff activity logs.
  • System Data: Login timestamps, device IP, browser type for security monitoring.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data solely to: process and track loans, generate branch daily reports, ensure repayment accountability, improve service delivery, and comply with financial regulations. We NEVER sell your data to third parties.

3. Data Security - Bank Grade Protection

  • 256-bit SSL/TLS Encryption in transit
  • Encrypted storage, hashed passwords (PBKDF2 + Salt)
  • CSRF, XSS, SQL Injection, Clickjacking protection enabled
  • Brute-force lockout after 5 failed attempts
  • Role-based access: CEO, Manager, Field Officer

4. Data Retention

Repayment history is retained permanently for audit and regulatory compliance. Daily branch reports are archived, not deleted, to protect financial integrity.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to request access, correction, or clarification of your data by contacting management through your branch.
